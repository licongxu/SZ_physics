# SZ paper index

**Hub:** [SZ Literature Map](SZ%20Literature%20Map.md) · **Derivation:** [Unified SZ Derivation](Unified%20SZ%20Derivation.md)

Not exhaustive. Landmark and 2019–2026 papers that define each probe. Topic notes in parentheses.

## Reviews (read these first)
- **Zeldovich & Sunyaev 1969; Sunyaev & Zeldovich 1970, 1972, 1980** — original tSZ / kSZ
- **Birkinshaw 1999**, Phys. Rep. 310, 97 — classic review
- **Carlstrom, Holder & Reese 2002**, ARA&A 40, 643
- **Kitayama 2014**, PTEP — cluster SZ
- **Mroczkowski et al. 2019**, SSRv 215, 17 — [arXiv:1811.02310](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.02310) — **best modern all-SZ review** (tSZ, kSZ, rSZ, ntSZ, polarised, multiple scattering, instruments)
- **Basu et al. 2019** — [arXiv:1903.04944](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.04944) — SZ spectral landscape
- **CMB secondaries 2025** — [arXiv:2501.13913](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13913)

## Theory: Compton hierarchy / operators
- Kompaneets 1956 — diffusion equation
- Wright 1979; Fabbri 1981; Rephaeli 1995 — relativistic kernel
- Challinor & Lasenby 1998; **Itoh, Kohyama & Nozawa 1998** [astro-ph/9712289](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9712289) — $`\theta_e`$ expansion ([rSZ literature](literature/rSZ%20literature.md))
- **Nozawa, Itoh & Kohyama 1998** [astro-ph/9804051](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9804051) — $`\beta\theta_e`$, $`\beta^2`$ ([rkSZ and higher kinematic](literature/rkSZ%20and%20higher%20kinematic.md))
- Sazonov & Sunyaev 1998, 1999 — kinematic + polarisation
- **Chluba et al. 2012** [arXiv:1205.5778](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.5778) — SZpack
- **Chluba et al. 2013** [arXiv:1211.3206](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.3206) — temperature–velocity moments
- Chluba, Dai & Kamionkowski 2014; Chluba & Dai 2014 — multiple scattering
- Chluba, Hütsi & Sunyaev 2005 — observer motion
- Chluba & Ravenni 2025 [arXiv:2505.02080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02080) — boost operator

- **Hoey, Long & Chluba 2026** [arXiv:2608.07404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07404) — kinematic corrections, all-orders ([Unified SZ Derivation](Unified%20SZ%20Derivation.md))
- Rosenberg & Chluba 2026 — polarised boost operator
- Relativistic-correction forecasts [arXiv:2504.18637](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18637)
