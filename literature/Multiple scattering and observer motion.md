# Multiple scattering and observer motion

**Hub:** [SZ Literature Map](../SZ%20Literature%20Map.md) · **Physics caveats:** [Unified SZ Derivation](../Unified%20SZ%20Derivation.md#assumptions-and-caveats)

Two corrections that are *not* extra $`(\theta_e,\beta)`$ cells of the single-scattering Maxwellian table.

---

## Multiple scattering ($`\tau^2`$, $`y\tau`$)

A photon can scatter more than once. Optical depth $`\tau\sim 0.01`$ so naive $`\tau^2\sim 10^{-4}`$, but the leading isotropic-scattering correction to tSZ is $`\sim 10\,y`$ times smaller still — about **$`0.1\%`$** of tSZ (Itoh+2001; Mroczkowski+2019).

Chluba, Dai & Kamionkowski (2014); Chluba & Dai (2014): anisotropic radiation + multiple scattering; in principle a handle on **3D** structure because second scatterings mix lines of sight. Polarisation from the local tSZ quadrupole is in this family ([Polarized SZ literature](Polarized%20SZ%20literature.md)).

**Detection:** none. Relevant only for 0.1%-level spectral work on the richest clusters.

---

## Observer motion

The derivation usually sets lab frame = CMB frame. The Solar System has $`\beta_{\odot}\simeq 1.24\times 10^{-3}`$ toward the CMB dipole.

Chluba, Hütsi & Sunyaev (2005); Nozawa et al. (2005): Lorentz transform the SZ signal into the observer frame. Effects:
- aberration and Doppler on cluster positions and spectra
- **dipolar modulation of SZ number counts** across the sky
- coupling to the CMB dipole spectrum (Balashev+2015)

Hoey+2026 explicitly omit this; they note it can be added as another boost.

**Detection:** not a standalone “observer-SZ detection”. The CMB dipole is known; this is a modelling term.

## Related small terms (for completeness)

- Scattering of **primordial CMB anisotropies** (not just the monopole) — dipole/quadrupole scattering, Chluba+2012. Sub-percent near the tSZ null.
- **Time delay / retardation** across a cluster.
- **Magnetic-field** deformation of the electron distribution (Koch+2003) — changes $`n_e`$, not a new Compton operator.

**Key papers:** Sunyaev & Zeldovich 1980 · Itoh+2001 · Chluba+2005 · Chluba, Dai & Kamionkowski 2014 · Chluba & Dai 2014. [SZ Paper Index](../SZ%20Paper%20Index.md)
