# Non-thermal SZ literature

**Hub:** [SZ Literature Map](../SZ%20Literature%20Map.md)

**Not a $(\theta_e,\beta)$ Maxwellian cell.** The electron distribution $f(p)$ is a power law, a $\kappa$-distribution, or a cosmic-ray tail. The Compton kernel then produces a **different** spectrum (ntSZ), usually rising more strongly into the submm than thermal rSZ.

---

## Background

Radio halos, relics, and AGN cocoons prove that clusters contain relativistic non-thermal electrons. Their pressure is typically **$\lesssim 1\%$** of thermal (Zandanel, Bartels, Pinzke). So ntSZ is a niche probe of **local** regions (bubbles, shocks), not of the global ICM.

Enßlin & Kaiser (2000); Colafrancesco, Marchegiani, Palladino (2003); Rephaeli (1995). Spectrum depends on the momentum cutoff and slope $p^{-s}$; submm bands ($\gtrsim 350\,\mathrm{GHz}$) are needed to separate ntSZ from tSZ/rSZ/kSZ/CIB (Mroczkowski+2019 Fig. 4; Basu+2019 landscape).

Also discussed: annihilating DM electrons (Colafrancesco 2004) — speculative.

---

## What people have done

- **Theory:** Compton kernel for power-law electrons; multi-component (thermal + non-thermal) fits.
- **Targets:** MS0735.6+7421, Hydra A, and other giant AGN cavities; radio relics.
- **Abdulla et al. (2018), Lacy et al. (2019):** SZ imaging of cavities / feedback; pressure in bubbles. Interpretation as *ntSZ spectrum* is not unique (geometry, thermal under-pressure, and CIB all conspire).
- **Simulations:** cosmic-ray MHD + mock SZ, still rare compared with thermal mocks.

---

## Detection significance

**No unambiguous ntSZ spectrum.** Cavity *deficits* in tSZ are seen and are important for feedback, but a unique non-thermal *spectral* detection is still missing.

**Open:** ALMA / NIKA2 / AtLAST spectroscopy of bubbles; joint radio synchrotron + ntSZ to pin $f(p)$.

**Key papers:** Rephaeli 1995 · Enßlin & Kaiser 2000 · Colafrancesco+2003 · Abdulla+2018 · Mroczkowski+2019 §2.4. [SZ Paper Index](../SZ%20Paper%20Index.md)
