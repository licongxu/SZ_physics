# Polarized SZ literature

**Hub:** [SZ Literature Map](../SZ%20Literature%20Map.md) · **Physics:** [Unified SZ Derivation](../Unified%20SZ%20Derivation.md#17-polarization)

Thomson scattering of a **quadrupole** produces linear polarisation $`Q\pm iU\sim\tau`$. Two quadrupoles matter.

---

## Two effects, often confused

| Name | Quadrupole source | Scaling | Spectrum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **pSZ** (remote / cosmological) | primordial CMB quadrupole as seen from the cluster | $`\tau\, a_{2m}(z)`$ | blackbody pol. |
| **kpSZ** (kinetic polarised) | boost of the CMB monopole → $`\mathcal{O}(\beta^2)`$ quadrupole | $`\tau\beta_\perp^2`$ | non-blackbody at higher order |
| thermal / multiple-scattering pol. | local intensity quadrupole from tSZ itself | $`\tau y`$, $`\tau^2`$ | $`y`$-like pol. |

Sazonov & Sunyaev (1999); Sunyaev & Zeldovich (1980); Kamionkowski & Loeb (1997): pSZ as a **remote quadrupole** snapshot — in principle maps our last-scattering quadrupole at other spacetime points (low-quadrupole anomaly test).

Relativistic polarisation: Nozawa, Itoh & Kohyama (1998, paper III); Challinor, Lasenby & others; Rosenberg & Chluba (2026) boost-operator polarised SZ.

## What people have done

### Theory / forecasts
- Kamionkowski & Loeb (1997) — cosmology from cluster polarisation
- Sazonov & Sunyaev (1999) — microwave polarisation toward clusters
- Hall & Challinor (2014) — detectability of quadrupole-induced pol.
- Deutsch, Johnson, Münchmeyer, … — pSZ tomography, remote dipole/quadrupole reconstruction
- Meyers, Meerburg, van Engelen, Battaglia (2018) — reionization $`\tau`$ beyond CMB cosmic variance with pSZ
- Deutsch et al. / Cayuso & Johnson — kSZ + pSZ to test CMB anomalies
- **kpSZ:** JCAP 2022 “Cosmology from the kinetic polarised SZ”; quadratic estimator for $`v_\perp^2`$; forecasts for SO/CMB-S4
- Rosenberg & Chluba (2026) — polarised boost-operator formalism

### Observations
**No detection of pSZ or kpSZ as of 2026.**

arXiv:2607.16071 (“Constraints on the remote quadrupole field from the polarised SZ effect”) applies a bispectrum / LSS-cross strategy to *current* data — constraints, not a claimed first detection. Amplitude is $`\sim\tau\times 10^{-5}`$ in polarisation (quadrupole $`\sim`$ few $`\mu\mathrm{K}`$, $`\tau\sim 10^{-2}`$), so stacking or tomography is required.

Cluster polarisation upper limits exist from various mm polarimeters; none isolate the remote quadrupole.

---

## Detection significance

**Zero detections.** Forecasts: first pSZ with next-generation CMB polarisation + dense galaxy surveys; kpSZ possibly later (smaller, $`\beta^2\sim 10^{-6}`$).

**Open:** first detection; EoR $`\tau`$ from pSZ tomography; leakage of Galactic dust and radio into cluster-centred stacks.
