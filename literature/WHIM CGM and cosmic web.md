# WHIM, CGM, and the cosmic web

**Hub:** [SZ Literature Map](../SZ%20Literature%20Map.md) · **tSZ:** [tSZ literature](tSZ%20literature.md) · **kSZ:** [kSZ literature](kSZ%20literature.md)

Lower-mass, lower-$T$ gas: groups, circumgalactic medium, filaments. Still Maxwellian tSZ/kSZ, but $y$ is $10^{-8}$–$10^{-6}$, so **only stacking** works today.

---

## Why it matters

$\Omega_b$ from BBN/CMB exceeds the baryons in stars + X-ray clusters. The rest is warm-hot ($10^5$–$10^7\,\mathrm{K}$) in filaments and CGM — faint in X-rays, visible in SZ if you stack.

kSZ is especially powerful: it does **not** need the gas to be hot, only ionized.

---

## What people have done

### Filaments (tSZ)
- Tanimura et al. (2019, MNRAS): Planck $y$ stacked on SDSS LRG pairs, residual filament **$y=(1.31\pm 0.25)\times 10^{-8}$ at $5.3\sigma$**.
- de Graaff et al. (2019): similar Planck filament stacks.
- Tanimura / de Graaff follow-ups; comparison to BAHAMAS.
- Cluster–cluster bridges in Planck (e.g. A399–A401) — rarer, individually claimed.

### Groups / CGM (tSZ + kSZ stacks)
- Planck tSZ × locally brightest galaxies (Planck 2013/2016).
- ACT + BOSS: Amodeo, Schaan, Battaglia, … **kSZ and tSZ profiles** around LRGs → gas extends beyond the dark-matter halo, AGN feedback.
- **Hadzhiyska et al. (2025)**: ACT + DESI, **$13\sigma$ kSZ**, strong evidence gas more spread out than DM; prefers stronger feedback than IllustrisTNG (see [kSZ literature](kSZ%20literature.md)).
- tSZ × weak lensing / galaxy density: Hill, Van Waerbeke, Koukoufilippas, … projected pressure.

### WHIM in $C_\ell$
Génova-Santos et al.: attempt to separate cluster vs WHIM tSZ in WMAP+SPT $C_\ell$. Controversial; modern analyses usually absorb this into the halo-model one-halo/two-halo + groups.

### Simulations
BAHAMAS, IllustrisTNG, EAGLE, **FLAMINGO** (McCarthy+2024 kSZ + tSZ–WL). The live question: kSZ wants **strong** feedback; some tSZ $C_\ell$ analyses want the same (Efstathiou & McCarthy 2025 vs fiducial FLAMINGO). *Pressure Profiles and tSZ PS*

## Detection significance

Filament tSZ: **stacked, $\sim 5\sigma$**. CGM kSZ: **high S/N (10$\sigma$ class)**. Individual WHIM filaments: generally **no**.

**Open:** photo-$z$ dilution; two-halo term vs bound CGM; consistent feedback story from kSZ, tSZ, kSZ×lensing, and X-ray.

**Key papers:** Planck LBG stacks · Tanimura+2019 · de Graaff+2019 · Schaan/Amodeo ACT+BOSS · Hadzhiyska+2025 · McCarthy+2024. [SZ Paper Index](../SZ%20Paper%20Index.md)
