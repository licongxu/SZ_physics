# rSZ literature

**Hub:** [SZ Literature Map](../SZ%20Literature%20Map.md) · **Physics:** [Unified SZ Derivation](../Unified%20SZ%20Derivation.md#8-rsz-is-the-next-thermal-orders) · **Bias:** *Hydrostatic Bias* (Kay+2024 $`T_y`$)

**Moment:** $`(m,k)=(0,2),(0,3),\ldots`$ → $`\int n_e T_e^{2}\,dl`$, $`\int n_e T_e^{3}\,dl`$. Same scattering as tSZ; next orders in $`\theta_e=kT_e/m_e c^2`$.

---

## Background

At $`kT_e\sim 5`$–$`10\,\mathrm{keV}`$, $`\theta_e\sim 0.01`$–$`0.02`$, so the Kompaneets $`y`$-spectrum is wrong at the **few-percent** level. The null shifts, the Wien tail rises, and the spectrum depends on **temperature moments**, not only pressure.

Wright (1979), Fabbri (1981), Rephaeli (1995): numerical Compton kernel. Challinor & Lasenby (1998), Itoh, Kohyama & Nozawa (1998), Sazonov & Sunyaev (1998): $`\theta_e`$ expansions. Chluba et al. (2012, 2013): SZpack + moment method $`y^{(k)}`$. Chluba & Rosenberg (2026), Hoey+2026: boost-operator all-orders.

The $`\theta_e`$ series is **asymptotic** (Wien tail). For hot clusters use SZpack, not a truncated $`Y_k`$.

## What people have done

### Theory / codes
Itoh+1998 $`Y_k`$ functions; Nozawa fits; SZpack (Chluba+2012); moment method (Chluba+2013) — LOS $`T_e`$ structure → extra spectral shapes; CLASS_SZ relativistic option; boost operators (2025–2026).

### Observations — stacked / statistical
Individual rSZ is a few % of tSZ, so surveys stack.

- Hansen+2002: early $`T_{\mathrm{SZ}}`$ claim
- Hurier (2016): “high significance” rSZ in Planck
- Erler, Basu, Chluba, Bertoldi (2018): Planck view of the SZ spectrum
- Remazeilles et al. (2025, MNRAS): residual rSZ in Planck *tSZ-free* CMB maps → $`\langle T_e\rangle\simeq 5\,\mathrm{keV}`$ for Planck clusters
- **Coulton et al. (ACT DR6 + Planck, PRD 2026)**: spectroscopic method, $`\langle T_e\rangle=8.5\pm 2.4\,\mathrm{keV}`$ for ACT $`y`$-selected clusters, **plus systematics of comparable size (passbands)**. Quotes previous Planck-cluster values $`4.4^{+2.1}_{-2.0}\,\mathrm{keV}`$ and $`4.9\pm 2.6\,\mathrm{keV}`$.

ACT clusters are hotter than Planck’s on average (higher-mass / better resolution at 150 GHz) — the two $`T_e`$ numbers should not be expected to match.

### Observations — individual
Butler et al. (2022): RX J1347.5−1145 relativistic correction. MACS J0717 is so hot ($`\sim 20\,\mathrm{keV}`$) that rSZ **must** be in the kSZ extraction, even if rSZ is not the science target.

Kay et al. (2024, FLAMINGO): $`y`$-weighted $`T_y`$ + GNFW → HSE masses; rSZ temperature as a mass-bias diagnostic. *Hydrostatic Bias*

### Forecasts
SO, CMB-S4, AtLAST, CMB-HD: spatially resolved $`T_e`$ from rSZ. Instrumental bandpasses already limit ACT (Coulton+2026).

---

## Detection significance

**Emerging, not mature.** Stacked $`T_e`$ is measured at a few $`\sigma`$, with systematics $`\sim`$ statistical error. Not yet a competitive mass proxy versus X-ray, but the spectrum *is* there.

---

## Open issues
Passbands and CIB; which temperature ($`T_y`$ vs $`T_{\mathrm{sl}}`$ vs $`T_m`$); ignoring rSZ biases $`Y`$ and ILC CMB maps; using rSZ $`T_e`$ in $`Y`$–$`M`$ cosmology.
